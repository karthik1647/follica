    5-Stage Decoupled Inference Architecture
    Combines deep neural representation with color-constancy-normalized computer vision and deterministic clinical safety gates.
    STAGE 00
    Pre-Inference Optical Gate (quality_gate.py)
    8x8 patch-max 2D Laplacian focus check (≥60), Rec.601 luminance bounds (35-225), and HSV specular flash-glare rejection (≤15% area).
    STAGE 01
    MobileNetV4-Conv-Small ONNX Classifier (classifier_service.py)
    10-class macroscopic pathology recognition in FP32 ONNX Runtime (<50 ms CPU latency) with dominance-margin calibration.
    STAGE 02
    Classical CV Micro-Feature Extractor (cv_pipeline.py)
    Gray-World white balance, CLAHE, YCrCb/HSV skin-hair segmentation, focal patch erythema contrast, and 6x6 bare-tile alopecia detection.
    STAGE 03
    Confidence Safety Gate & Escalation Matrix (triage_engine.py)
    Predictions below 0.65 confidence are intercepted server-side to Level 2 Indeterminate; scarring, fungal, and pustular patterns escalate to Level 3.
    STAGE 04
    Post-Identification Trigger Engine & Clinical PDF (survey_risk_engine.py)
    Ranks modifiable environmental/systemic drivers conditioned on the identified pattern and exports a 1-page generic ReportLab physician handoff pass.

    Frontiers in Medicine · 2022 · DOI: 10.3389/fmed.2022.981884
    Deep Learning Differentiation of Scalp Psoriasis vs. Seborrheic Dermatitis

    Demonstrated CNN trichoscopic/macroscopic discrimination between overlapping erythematous-squamous scalp disorders (AUC 0.922, sensitivity 96.1%, specificity 88.2%), outperforming non-specialist visual inspection.

    Follica Implementation: Motivates pairing neural class probabilities with explicit plaque/flake and focal erythema sub-scores to separate Psoriasis (Level 3 escalation) from Seborrheic Dermatitis (Level 2 monitored).
    Computers in Biology and Medicine / IEEE Access · 2022-2023
    Lightweight MobileNet & EfficientNet Backbones for Multi-Class Scalp Pathology

    Benchmarked depthwise-separable convolutional architectures across multi-class scalp lesions (folliculitis, alopecia, fungal tinea, dermatitis), achieving >91% multi-class accuracy at smartphone inference budgets.

    Follica Implementation: Grounds our selection of timm MobileNetV4-Conv-Small two-stage transfer learning exported to standalone ONNX Runtime for sub-50 ms CPU execution.
    Scientific Reports / Journal of Dermatological Science · 2023
    Automated Trichoscopic Hair Coverage & Alopecia Feature Segmentation

    Established morphological dark-fiber segmentation and spatial follicular void analysis for grading patchy Alopecia Areata (AUC 0.97) and androgenetic vertex thinning.

    Follica Implementation: Directly informs our compute_density_score morphological Black-Hat fiber mask and 6x6 interior bare-skin tile detector for focal scarring/patchy alopecia voids.
    Sensors / MDPI Diagnostics · 2023
    Color-Constancy Normalization & Hybrid CV-Neural Fusion Under Ambient Lighting

    Academic Transparency & Known Limitations 1. Dataset Cross-Split Caveat: Near-100% clean test accuracy partially reflects pre-augmented image similarity in the public Kaggle Hair Diseases benchmark; real-world smartphone captures exhibit lower confidence, which is why our 0.65 server-side Confidence Gate is strictly enforced.
