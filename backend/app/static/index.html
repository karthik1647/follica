    Pre-Inference Optical Gateway: Why We Added It
    The clinical, mathematical, and Stanford Biodesign rationale for enforcing an upfront optical rejection firewall.
    THE CLINICAL AI PARADOX: GARBAGE IN, CONFIDENT GARBAGE OUT Deep convolutional networks are trained on clean datasets and will greedily compute softmax probability distributions over whatever tensor they are fed. If a patient uploads a blurry, pitch-black, or flash-washed photo, an unconstrained neural network will still output a high-confidence prediction — falsely confusing camera flash for silvery psoriasis plaques, or motion blur for patchy alopecia.
    PROBLEM 1
    Camera Flash Specular Glare Mimics Severe Flaking
    Curved scalp skin acts as a specular reflector. Direct smartphone camera flash produces saturated white hotspots (luminance > 240, chroma < 20) that blind color sensors and corrupt classical morphological flake filtering.
    PROBLEM 2
    Motion Blur Erases Micro-Feature Edges
    Hand tremor or close-range focal failure attenuates high-frequency spatial gradients, erasing follicular openings and perifollicular erythema halos essential for separating Seborrheic Dermatitis from Lichen Planus.
    SOLUTION
    Refusal to Guess & Immediate Constructive Guidance
    The Photo Gateway intercepts frames before model evaluation. If physical criteria are breached, it rejects the photo with HTTP 400 and actionable steps ("Wipe lens, use diffuse room light, 10-15 cm distance"), guaranteeing clinical reliability.
    What Does The Photo Gateway Do? (Technical Defense Suite)
    Real-time deterministic filters executed in volatile RAM before neural classification:
    Gateway Filter Mathematical Threshold Failure Mode Prevented
    Laplacian Focus ↗ 8x8 patch-max Var ≥ 60.0 Rejects motion & out-of-focus blur
    Rec.601 Luminance ↗ 35.0 ≤ Y_mean ≤ 225.0 Rejects underexposure & blowout
    HSV Flash Glare ↗ Specular Area ≤ 15.0% Rejects flash-hotspot false dandruff
    Spatial Envelope ↗ ≥ 200 x 200 pixels Rejects low-res thumbnails
    Ephemeral RAM ↗ Zero disk / cloud persistence Protects patient biometric privacy

In our automated Persona 5 test suite, it achieves 100% rejection on blurred, underexposed, and flash-washed smartphone failures.

    Dermatologist / Clinician
    Does Follica attempt to diagnose patients or prescribe pharmacological regimens?
    Never. Follica operates strictly as a pre-consultation triage and anamnesis structuring tool. It never outputs definitive diagnoses or prescription drugs (topical steroids, oral antifungals, finasteride, or antibiotics). Scarring, autoimmune, fungal, pustular, and plaque patterns automatically trigger Level 3 Specialist Escalation with a 1-page clinical handoff summary to streamline dermatology visits.
    Dermatologist / Clinician
    Smartphone cameras lack polarized magnification — how do you avoid missing Psoriasis or Scarring Alopecia?
    Unpolarized RGB cannot resolve subsurface peripilar casts or vascular patterns (dotted vs. arborizing vessels). We address this via asymmetric clinical risk routing: whenever Psoriasis or Lichen Planus crosses our confidence gate — or when silvery crusting, burning trichodynia, or painful pustules are reported — triage_engine.py forces Level 3 Escalation for confirmatory polarized dermoscopy.
    Dermatologist / Clinician
    Why are questionnaire answers excluded from the Scalp Health Score (SHS)?
    Blending subjective self-reports (stress, diet) into an optical score corrupts longitudinal measurement validity — an anxious patient with calm skin would falsely score as inflamed. Keeping SHS (0-100) purely image-derived across 4 SDASI/SALT-inspired axes while running a separate Post-Identification Trigger Engine preserves clean clinical separation between visible signs and historical cofactors.
    Professor / Biodesign Jury
    Isn't 100% test accuracy on the 12,000-image dataset inflated by cross-split augmentation?
    Yes — and we explicitly audit and report this caveat. Our training notebook includes perceptual-hash leakage checks and notes that pre-augmented public benchmarks overestimate wild smartphone accuracy. Consequently, we validate under out-of-distribution geometric/photometric perturbations (97.0% accuracy), enforce a 0.65 server-side Confidence Gate, and document CV brackets (thresholds.md) as provisional heuristics awaiting prospective IRB calibration.
    Professor / Biodesign Jury
    What makes this a genuine Biodesign system rather than a generic CNN wrapper?
    Every module traces directly to a need-specification constraint: Stage 00 Optical Gate prevents bad patient self-captures; Stage 03 Confidence Abstention + Level-3 Override manages clinical false-negative risk; Stage 04 Layer-2 Survey Engine converts campus/hostel exposures (hard borewell water, helmet sweat occlusion) into ranked interventions; Molecule-First output removes D2C prescribing bias; ORB/RANSAC registration closes the 14-day follow-up loop.
    Professor / Biodesign Jury
    How are pediatric safety, informed consent, and biometric data retention governed?
    Selecting age <18 activates a mandatory Guardian & Pediatric Physician Notice (critical since pediatric Tinea Capitis requires systemic care). Raw scalp photos are decoded solely in volatile RAM and discarded immediately after HTTP response; SQLite consent logs store only salted SHA-256 client pseudonyms with instant DELETE right-to-erasure.
    ML / Systems Engineer
    Why pair MobileNetV4 ONNX with classical OpenCV instead of calling a cloud Vision-LLM?
    Multimodal LLMs introduce hallucination risk, 3-6 second API latency, external biometric transmission, and non-repeatable numeric estimates. MobileNetV4-Conv-Small in FP32 ONNX Runtime executes locally on CPU in <50 ms (~10 MB footprint), while deterministic OpenCV provides pixel-reproducible sub-scores and ORB/RANSAC perspective alignment.
    ML / Systems Engineer
    How does the pipeline defend against blurry frames, flash hotspots, or out-of-distribution photos?
    Stage 00 (quality_gate.py) enforces 8x8 patch-max 2D Laplacian sharpness (≥60.0), Rec.601 luminance (35-225), and HSV specular glare area (≤15%), returning HTTP 400 with retake guidance before inference. Any ambiguous image that passes optical checks but yields dispersed logits is caught by dominance-margin calibration and the 0.65 Confidence Gate.
    ML / Systems Engineer
    How do you prevent warm indoor bulbs or darker Fitzpatrick skin tones from biasing Redness scores?
    Raw RGB R-channel thresholds fail across diverse skin phototypes and warm lighting. Instead, cv_pipeline.py applies clamped Gray-World color constancy followed by relative chrominance contrast — measuring top-quartile focal erythema relative to the subject's own median scalp skin tone while masking hair shafts and specular glare pixels.
    Patient / Student
    Are my scalp photographs stored on a server or used to train AI models?
    No. Your photograph is processed in temporary server memory (RAM) for a fraction of a second to calculate your report and is permanently discarded the moment your assessment appears. Nothing is saved to disk, cloud buckets, or external databases.
    Patient / Student
    Why does Follica recommend generic molecules (like Ketoconazole) instead of shampoo brands?
    Many online hair tests exist solely to sell expensive bundled kits. Follica has zero commercial affiliations: we show the evidence-backed OTC active molecule, concentration, and contact schedule (with direct NIH PubChem references) so you or your pharmacist/dermatologist can choose any affordable, accessible formulation.
    Patient / Student
    When should I skip home care and book an in-person dermatologist immediately?
    Seek prompt specialist care whenever Follica indicates Level 3: Clinical Escalation, or if you notice painful bumps/pustules, thick silvery plaques, sudden smooth patchy bald spots, scalp burning/tenderness, or no improvement after 2-4 weeks of gentle routine care. Use Export Physician PDF to bring structured notes to your appointment.
